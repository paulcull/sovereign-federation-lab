RESTRICTION EXAMPLE

Blocked access can precede physical purge.

Singapore remains the authority. At t=5s it stops new UK contact exports. With the assumed healthy guard path, the UK applies revision 8 at t=5.20s and denies affected reads/imports. Existing contact copies can remain pending deletion.

A packet already in flight is governed by the agreed acknowledgement/lease window. Source commit is not simultaneous worldwide enforcement. Missing authority affects only the relevant scopes; unrelated authorised work continues.

Independent lanes do not imply independent authorisation.

RESOURCE ISOLATION

Separate the capacity as well as the queues.

  • Independent outbox claim paths, regional topics, worker groups and connection budgets.
  • Small guard transactions; no scan or backfill inside policy activation.
  • Bounded bulk transactions, byte/IOPS limits and per-entity fairness.
  • Current-policy validation at export, import and read; monotonic fences and data versions.
  • Separate SLOs for record visibility, restriction enforcement and physical convergence.
Assumptions, limits and how to reproduce

Deterministic arrivals and one unit of service per task. Total capacity is 1,000 units/s. Guard reserves 50 in both designs. The shared FIFO receives a single policy batch ahead of later operational arrivals. Isolated pools reserve 700 for operations and 250 for bulk, without idle-capacity borrowing. Each operational delivery adds a fixed assumed 60ms; a guard message adds 180ms transport and 20ms service.

Quantiles cover operations arriving from t=5s through t<60s. This is a scheduling illustration, not a database performance forecast. It excludes CPU/disk contention, cache misses, retries and variable task sizes. Separate queues cannot eliminate shared-disk or hot-key contention. The production acceptance target is existing SLOs plus ≤10% operational p99 degradation during bulk work, to be validated experimentally.

Run node scale/isolation_test.mjs from the source download. The admission specification checks stale policy, old data, placement/home epochs, authority gaps and stale-purge races. It is not an OPA or distributed-consistency implementation.
