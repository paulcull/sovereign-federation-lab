# ADR-0004: Minimal global party identity and scoped relationship graphs

Recorded: 11 October 2026. Retrospective project decision; not a bank governance approval.

## Status

Accepted; normalized example implemented

## Context

Party identity may be shared across operations while relationship, holding and servicing data remain entity/policy scoped.

## Decision

Treat minimal party identity as a distinct eligible scope. Apply table defaults and party/relationship/holding-key restrictions to the business graph. A global ID does not imply global personal data.

## Alternatives

Replicate every party attribute globally; federate only whole customer documents.

## Consequences

Different scopes can share source rows but produce different field projections. Validate parent closure and retain owner-qualified keys.

## Evidence

shared/operational.json; poc/operational_schema.sql; poc/operational_core.py

## Gate

US sees only explicitly permitted party minimum; relationship and contact exclusions remain effective.
