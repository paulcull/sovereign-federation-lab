# ADR-0017: Existing business services with governed peer integration and routing

Recorded: 12 October 2026. Proposed pilot decision; not a bank governance approval.

## Status

Proposed pilot design; explanatory contracts only, independent peers and automatic routing not implemented

## Context

Many banking services already exist. The sovereignty layer should simplify approved peer deployment and govern location and movement while business services retain interactions and data ownership.

## Decision

Reuse existing business APIs and models through bounded adapters. Integrate only services reading or mutating governed shared data; cover every writer of that data with a compatible atomic outbox or local transaction hook, versions and mandatory audit. Deploy authenticated regional sovereignty peers alongside participating services. A gateway or adapter consumes trusted versioned placement: writes target current owner authority; reads use an authorised projection with acceptable freshness or a lawful owner path for latest state. Recheck permissions and authority at the handler.

## Alternatives

Rewrite every banking service; require estate-wide consolidation; route all payloads through a global sovereignty proxy; trust raw CDC or cached routes as complete enforcement; write through recipient projections.

## Consequences

Loose coupling separates remote delivery from business commit, but does not remove the local mutation and guarded-read contracts. Routing never grants access and processing location is checked before payload forwarding. Fail closed on unknown authority or mandatory policy failure; do not use nearest-store fallback or promote a writer. Stale routes after migration cannot bypass a durable fence. Shared deployment does not union entity rights.

## Gate

Before pilot, deploy independent authenticated peers against an existing-service adapter; verify atomic writer coverage, permitted payload routes, current-policy projection reads, explicit freshness, authority loss, stale placement and fenced cutover under crash/partition. The current one-process SQL/OPA lab and isolated migration are evidence only for their implemented subsets.
